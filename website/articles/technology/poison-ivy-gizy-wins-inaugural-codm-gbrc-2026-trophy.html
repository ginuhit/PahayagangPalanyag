Poison Ivy Gizy Wins Inaugural CODM GBRC 2026 Trophy

Palanyag Reporter•November 11, 2026

Poison Ivy Gizy emerged as the champion of the inaugural CODM GBRC 2026, securing the first-ever trophy for the tournament. The victory highlights Gizy's skill in the competitive Call of Duty Mobile (CODM) scene.

The event served as a major milestone for mobile esports, showcasing high-level gameplay and strategic mastery. Gizy’s win solidifies their position as a top contender in the evolving gaming landscape.
