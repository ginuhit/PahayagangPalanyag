The Logistics of Illicit Trade: Why a Butuan Bust Matters to Parañaque

Photo courtesy of Police Regional Office 13

On November 8, 2026, law enforcement in Butuan City intercepted a significant shipment of suspected shabu. The arrest of a 30-year-old suspect, identified by the alias “Cyclone,” resulted in the seizure of 104.75 grams of the illicit substance—a haul valued at over P700,000. While the operation took place in the Caraga Region, the implications of such an arrest ripple far beyond the provincial borders of Agusan del Sur, touching the very heart of the National Capital Region’s security landscape.
