Lopez Heirs Reorganize Empire: Crème Investment Corp Acquires Rockwell Land in P15.92-B Deal

By Palanyag Reporter • November 15, 2026

Rappler

Crème Investment Corporation, the private firm led by Eugenio “Gabby” Lopez III and his siblings, has finalized a deal to acquire a 90.74% voting stake in Rockwell Land from First Philippine Holdings Corporation (FPH) for P15.92 billion. Announced on Thursday, November 12, the transaction completes a complex 94-day asset swap intended to realign the family's resources toward their specific business missions.

This agreement follows the August 10 sale of Crème’s 25.68% interest in the family’s primary holding company, Lopez Inc., to San Miguel Corporation head Ramon S. Ang for approximately P11 billion. The maneuver allows the Lopez heirs to decouple their interests, effectively separating consumer-facing lifestyle and media brands from the heavy infrastructure and energy portfolio managed by Federico “Piki” Lopez.

The acquisition serves as a calculated move to provide financial stability for Gabby Lopez’s business interests. While Rockwell Land remains a powerhouse of profitability—generating P4.73 billion in 2025—ABS-CBN has struggled with a P4.72 billion loss during the same period. By securing Rockwell, Crème gains a reliable revenue stream to fuel its mission of rebuilding ABS-CBN, a network Gabby describes as the heartbeat of the family's mission to serve the Filipino people. The network is currently navigating a P6-billion fundraising drive, to which Crème has already contributed P766.7 million.

The deal underscores a clear split in the family's business philosophy. Piki Lopez’s leadership at FPH and First Gen is defined by long-term, capital-intensive energy projects, such as geothermal and hydropower, where "direction over speed" and "compounding value" are the guiding principles. Conversely, Gabby Lopez is consolidating assets that cater to the everyday Filipino consumer. This includes the media presence of ABS-CBN and the luxury lifestyle ecosystem of Rockwell Land, which transformed an old Meralco power plant site in Makati into the premier Rockwell Center, home to the Power Plant Mall, high-end condominiums, and offices.

The transfer of Rockwell also carries significant historical weight. The company has long been linked to the late Manuel “Manolo” Lopez, the former head of Meralco. By bringing Rockwell under Crème’s direct control, the heirs are reclaiming a business asset that resonates with Manolo’s side of the family, particularly following recent internal challenges to the current leadership of FPH.

For the public, the shift signals a more specialized approach to corporate management within the Lopez group. While the energy sector remains under the steady, long-term stewardship of FPH, the consumer and media sectors will now be driven by a group focused on immediate consumer engagement and the revitalization of national broadcasting.
