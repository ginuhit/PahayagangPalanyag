UST Growling Tigers Secure Dominant Win Over UE Red Warriors to Fuel Final Four Push

Palanyag Reporter•November 12, 2026

INQUIRER.net

The University of Santo Tomas (UST) Growling Tigers solidified their pursuit of a Final Four berth with a commanding 97-70 victory over the University of the East (UE) Red Warriors on Tuesday at the Smart Araneta Coliseum. The win was anchored by the impressive debut of Nigerian forward Peter Osang, who replaced Collins Akowe as the team’s foreign student-athlete (FSA). Osang’s immediate impact provided the physical presence and energy the coaching staff sought to counter the high-tempo style of the Red Warriors.

Osang, a 6-foot-7 big man, dominated the interior, recording 19 points, 10 rebounds, and five blocks. The Tigers established an early and insurmountable lead, racing to a 35-19 advantage in the first quarter alone. While Osang was a focal point, the offensive spread was evident as Amiel Acido led the scoring with 22 points, supported by Landyn Jumawan’s 16 points and Mark Llemit’s 11-point contribution. Despite a valiant effort from UE’s Toper Lagat, who notched 22 points, the Red Warriors were unable to overcome the Tigers' disciplined defensive rotation.

This victory comes at a critical juncture for both programs. For the Red Warriors, the loss served as a harsh reality check following their recent triumph that snapped a grueling 30-game losing streak spanning over two seasons. Conversely, for the Growling Tigers, the win is a vital step toward survival. The team now sits within half a game of the Final Four circle, currently occupied by host Far Eastern University and De La Salle University, both boasting 6-5 records.

Assistant coach Peter Martin emphasized the "do-or-die" nature of the remaining schedule, noting that a single loss could eliminate the team from title contention. The successful integration of Osang suggests that UST has found the missing piece to match the league’s most aggressive offenses. As the elimination round enters its homestretch, the Tigers must maintain this level of discipline and composure to ensure their seat at the table for the postseason.
