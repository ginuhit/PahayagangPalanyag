Adamson Falcons Snap Losing Streak with Gritty Victory Over Ateneo

Palanyag Reporter•November 11, 2026

The Adamson Falcons halted a four-game losing streak on Wednesday, securing a 62-55 victory over the Ateneo Blue Eagles at the Smart Araneta Coliseum. The win serves as a critical turning point in the UAAP Season 89 men’s basketball tournament, reviving the Falcons' aspirations to secure a spot in the Final Four.

The Falcons established a steady lead, finishing the first half at 28-25 before pulling away with a dominant 19-11 run in the third quarter to create a 47-36 cushion. While the Blue Eagles attempted a late-game surge—narrowing the deficit to five points during a fourth-quarter run led by Shawn Tuano and Sultan Baruwa—Adamson’s defense remained impenetrable. A pivotal factor in the win was the return of Precious Jonah, who overcame a right leg injury to deliver a massive double-double of 11 points and 17 rebounds, complemented by three blocks and two steals. Manu Anabo also provided a late spark, converting a four-point play with 3:45 remaining to seal the victory.

This win is more than just a statistical improvement; it is a psychological reset for a team that had struggled to find its rhythm following a demoralizing loss to the University of the East. Head coach Nash Racela emphasized the team's need to overcome a "sleeping" tendency after consecutive defeats. By rebounding from that "painful" loss, the Falcons have demonstrated the resilience required to compete at the highest level. Currently sitting at 5-7, the Falcons are tied with Ateneo for sixth place but remain within 1.5 games of third-place contenders Far Eastern University and La Salle. For the Adamson community, this result reaffirms the team's ability to withstand pressure and remain a viable threat in the standings.

Looking ahead, the Falcons face a daunting challenge against La Salle this coming Sunday at 2:30 p.m. Meanwhile, the Blue Eagles will take on the fifth-running University of Santo Tomas. Both programs will be looking to leverage this weekend's momentum to determine their ultimate fate in the tournament.
