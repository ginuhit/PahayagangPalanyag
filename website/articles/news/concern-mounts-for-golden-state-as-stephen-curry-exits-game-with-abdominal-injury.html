Concern Mounts for Golden State as Stephen Curry Exits Game with Abdominal Injury

By Palanyag Reporter • November 13, 2026

INQUIRER.net

HOUSTON — The Golden State Warriors’ momentum faced a sudden and unsettling halt on Wednesday night when superstar Stephen Curry was forced to leave the court during the first quarter of their matchup against the Houston Rockets. The 38-year-old veteran, who has been anchoring the team’s recent resurgence, left the game citing right abdominal soreness, sparking immediate concern regarding his availability for the upcoming stretch of the season.

The incident unfolded shortly after Curry successfully converted a three-pointer, marking his only basket of the evening. As he backpedaled following the shot, the two-time NBA MVP winced and visibly clutched his right side. Observers noted that Curry appeared to be in significant discomfort, walking gingerly toward the locker room while holding his abdomen. He did not return to the court for the remainder of the game, leaving the Warriors to navigate the rest of the contest without their primary offensive engine.

Head coach Steve Kerr confirmed the gravity of the situation, noting that Curry had expressed confusion over the sudden sensation. “He said he tweaked something in his abdominal area and said he’s never done it before,” Kerr told reporters following the game. The situation escalated when Curry left the arena during the second half to undergo an MRI. While the medical staff has yet to release official imaging results, Kerr did not mince words regarding the uncertainty of the injury. “I mean, concern is high, but I have no idea the extent of the injury,” Kerr stated.

The absence of their talismanic leader had an immediate impact on the scoreboard. Without Curry’s scoring prowess to stabilize the offense, the Warriors struggled to maintain their rhythm, ultimately falling to the Rockets with a final score of 115-108. This defeat effectively snapped Golden State’s impressive eight-game winning streak, a run that had provided much-needed hope following a disastrous 0-3 start to the season.

For the Warriors organization, the implications of this injury extend far beyond a single loss. Curry entered the night as one of the most dominant forces in the league, currently ranked second in NBA scoring with an average of 30.6 points per game. His ability to carry the offensive load has been the cornerstone of the team's recent success, serving as the leading scorer in six of their last eight victories. As the team navigates the grueling mid-season schedule, the health of their 12-time All-Star becomes the central narrative for both the coaching staff and a fanbase eager to see this veteran squad remain competitive for a championship. Until the MRI results provide clarity, the Warriors remain in a state of anxious limbo.
