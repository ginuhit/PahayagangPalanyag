Andreeva Stuns Sabalenka to Secure WTA Finals Semifinal Berth

By Palanyag Reporter • November 13, 2026

Inquirer Sports

Russian sensation Mirra Andreeva has stormed into the WTA Finals semifinals, delivering a masterclass in resilience by defeating Aryna Sabalenka 6-4, 6-0 on Wednesday. The 19-year-old, who recently captured her maiden major at Roland Garros, overcame a shaky start to dismantle the world number two in a match that lasted just 62 minutes.

The victory was a testament to Andreeva's mental fortitude during her singles debut at this elite season-ending event. After falling behind 4-1 in the opening set, the world number three appeared to be grappling with the immense pressure of the big stage. A double fault that led to a lost break initially seemed to put her at a disadvantage against Sabalenka's heavy-hitting style. However, Andreeva quickly recalibrated, neutralizing the Belarusian's powerful serves with aggressive groundstrokes that pushed her opponent back and forced uncharacteristic errors.

The second set saw Andreeva take complete control, showcasing a level of dominance rarely seen against a player of Sabalenka's caliber. She secured an early break for a 3-0 lead, and although Sabalenka managed to save three break points while trailing 0-40, the momentum had already shifted irrevocably. Andreeva secured another decisive break with a clinical swinging volley winner, followed by a Sabalenka forehand that sailed long. The Belarusian eventually succumbed to the mounting pressure, hitting a backhand into the net to hand match point to the teenager, who closed the match with a decisive service winner.

"I’m even happier than I was after my first match," Andreeva remarked following the win. "I feel like I can play another two matches, I’m so pumped up. I’m so happy."

For Sabalenka, the defeat is a frustrating chapter in a season that has seen her claim titles in Indian Wells and Miami but fail to secure a Grand Slam trophy. Despite the lopsided second set, the 28-year-old is not yet out of the competition. She maintains a path to the semifinals provided she can secure a victory against Elina Svitolina in their final round-robin match this Friday.

Meanwhile, Elina Svitolina kept her own campaign alive by fighting back from a set down to overcome Karolina Muchova 3-6, 6-2, 6-2. The Ukrainian veteran, who hasn't returned to this elite event since 2019, struggled early as Muchova broke her three times in the opening set. Svitolina, a former WTA Finals champion from 2018, credited her comeback to tactical adjustments and a necessary shift toward a more aggressive playing style, proving that her experience remains a vital asset in high-stakes tournament play.

The results of Wednesday's matches have reshaped the tournament's final bracket. Muchova’s second consecutive loss in the round-robin stage, combined with Andreeva’s dominant victory, officially eliminates the Czech player from semifinal contention. As the tournament progresses, the rise of Andreeva as a dominant force in the women's game continues to shift the landscape of professional tennis, signaling a potential changing of the guard in the upper echelons of the WTA.
