Choco Mucho Flying Titans Break Seven-Game Skid Against Creamline Cool Smashers

By Palanyag Reporter • November 12, 2026

INQUIRER.net

The Choco Mucho Flying Titans clinched a grueling victory against their sister team, the Creamline Cool Smashers, during the 2026 PVL All-Filipino Conference Siklab Series on Thursday at the Smart Araneta Coliseum in Manila. This pivotal win allowed the Flying Titans to maintain their aspirations for a spot in the semifinals, showcasing a resilient and determined performance in a high-stakes environment. The match was a marathon display of athleticism and tactical strategy, ultimately ending in a triumph for Choco Mucho as they managed to overcome the formidable Cool Smashers in a contest that tested the endurance and mental fortitude of both squads.

The final score of the match—23-25, 25-13, 25-23, 22-25, 15-13—highlights the intense back-and-forth nature of the competition. The Flying Titans were forced into a deciding fifth set, where they demonstrated a heightened sense of urgency following previous losses that had left them in a precarious position. Eya Laure emerged as a pivotal figure in the closing moments, delivering the telling blows that thwarted Creamline’s attempt at a comeback for the match point. Additionally, Tots Carlos played a crucial role by saving a critical point, while Caitlin Viray, despite an initial attack error, redeemed herself by delivering the game-winning kill. These individual performances were instrumental in securing the victory and ensuring that Choco Mucho moved forward in the tournament.

This victory holds significant historical weight as it marks the Choco Mucho Flying Titans' second win in their 21-match history against the Creamline Cool Smashers. Prior to this match, the Flying Titans had endured a frustrating seven-game losing streak against their sister team, a dynamic that has often defined their rivalry. The fact that Choco Mucho’s first win against Creamline only occurred in the 2024 semifinals underscores the dominance Creamline has historically maintained over the sister club. By breaking this skid, Choco Mucho has signaled a shift in the competitive balance between the two organizations, providing a boost to the team's morale and standing within the league.

The impact of this result extends beyond the immediate scoreboard, as it reshapes the standings of the 2026 PVL All-Filipino Conference Siklab Series. With this win, Choco Mucho improved their record to 4-3, currently sitting in fourth place and positioning themselves for a potential semifinal berth. Conversely, the loss dropped Creamline to a 4-2 record, tying them with Farm Fresh. This shift in momentum creates a narrative of rising strength for Choco Mucho and a narrative of mounting pressure for Creamline. Such matches serve as a catalyst for fan engagement and heighten the intensity of the league, as the community watches these two powerhouse teams vie for supremacy in the domestic volleyball landscape.

Looking ahead, both teams face a challenging schedule as they attempt to secure their respective goals for the remainder of the conference. The Choco Mucho Flying Titans are scheduled to face the PLDT High Speed Hitters, who currently hold a 3-3 record, next Thursday at the Philsports Arena. This match will be critical in determining whether they can officially seal their spot in the semifinals. Meanwhile, the Creamline Cool Smashers must find a way to bounce back quickly from their recent setback. They are set to face the semis-bound Nxled on Tuesday at the Mall of Asia Arena before taking on Farm Fresh on November 21. The upcoming week will be a defining period for both teams as they navigate the final hurdles of the conference.
