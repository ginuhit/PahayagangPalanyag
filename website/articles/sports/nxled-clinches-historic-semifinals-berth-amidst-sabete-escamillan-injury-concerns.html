Nxled Clinches Historic Semifinals Berth Amidst Sabete-Escamillan Injury Concerns

By Palanyag Reporter • November 13, 2026

Inquirer Sports

The Nxled Chameleons etched their names into the history books on Thursday, securing a monumental milestone by clinching their first-ever semifinals berth in the 2026 PVL All-Filipino Conference Siklab Series. The victory, achieved at the iconic Smart Araneta Coliseum, marks a significant turning point for a franchise that has long been searching for its breakthrough moment in the premier volleyball circuit. However, the jubilation surrounding the achievement was immediately tempered by a moment of profound concern when veteran wing spiker Jonah Sabete-Escamillan suffered a significant leg injury during a high-stakes match against Akari.

The match itself was a grueling test of nerves, with the Chameleons navigating a series of tightly contested sets to emerge victorious with scores of 27-25, 25-22, and 26-24. This win extended their impressive undefeated streak to six games, but the momentum was abruptly halted midway through the third set. The atmosphere in the arena shifted from electric to somber as Sabete-Escamillan experienced an awkward landing, leaving her unable to continue. The sight of the veteran being carried off the court on a stretcher cast a shadow over the proceedings, leaving the coaching staff and fans in a state of collective anxiety. Head coach Ettore Guidetti admitted that the victory felt bittersweet, describing it as perhaps one of the saddest wins of his professional career.

While the immediate aftermath was filled with tension, initial medical assessments have provided a glimmer of cautious optimism. Preliminary tests and physical therapy sessions conducted on Sabete-Escamillan’s left leg have yielded negative results, suggesting that the injury may not be as catastrophic as initially feared. Nevertheless, Guidetti has urged the team and the public to remain measured. He emphasized that the organization is not relying solely on these early findings, noting that a more comprehensive medical investigation is scheduled for the coming days to provide absolute certainty regarding her status for the remainder of the tournament.

In the wake of Sabete-Escamillan’s sudden exit, the Chameleons were forced to pivot instantly. EJ Laure-Cariño, the former University of Santo Tomas standout, rose to the occasion with remarkable poise. Delivering a crucial nine-point performance, Laure-Cariño provided the offensive spark needed to maintain the lead, punctuated by a decisive, game-clinching attack. As a long-tenured member of the roster, Laure-Cariño underscored the necessity of mental and physical readiness within the league's unique all-to-play format. She noted that the team’s ability to adapt has been essential, as the roster is built to ensure that any player can step into a leadership role at a moment's notice.

This resilience is a testament to the growing chemistry within the Nxled squad. The seamless integration of players such as Brooke Van Sickle and MJ Phillips, alongside the seasoned presence of former Cignal duo Gel Cayuna and Jackie Acuña, has forged a formidable and versatile roster. This synergy has allowed the Chameleons to maintain their momentum even when facing significant adversity. As the tournament progresses, Nxled must now navigate high-stakes matchups against powerhouse teams Creamline and ZUS Coffee. While the team celebrates their historic advancement, the focus remains on both the health of Sabete-Escamillan and the pursuit of their ultimate goal: a PVL championship.
