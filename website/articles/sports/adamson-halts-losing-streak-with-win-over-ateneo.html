Adamson Halts Losing Streak with Win Over Ateneo

Palanyag Reporter•November 11, 2026

Adamson University snapped its four-game losing streak after securing a victory against Ateneo de Manila University. The Falcons' turnaround was fueled by a standout performance from Precious Jonah, who played a pivotal role in leading the team to the win.

The victory provides Adamson with much-needed momentum as they seek to climb the standings. By defeating a formidable Ateneo, the Falcons successfully halted their recent slump and solidified their position in the tournament.
