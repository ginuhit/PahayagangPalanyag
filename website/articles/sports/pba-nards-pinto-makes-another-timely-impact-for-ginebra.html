PBA: Nards Pinto makes another timely impact for Ginebra

Palanyag Reporter•November 11, 2026

Nards Pinto delivered a timely performance for Barangay Ginebra San Miguel in their recent PBA outing. Pinto’s versatile play and ability to contribute during critical moments provided a significant boost to the team's offensive efforts.

His contributions in both scoring and rebounding helped Ginebra maintain momentum against their opponents. Pinto continues to prove his reliability as a key rotation player who can step up when the team needs it most.
